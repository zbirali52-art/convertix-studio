* **Core Pricing:** 
  * 🇮🇳 **India:** Flat **₹1,000 INR** (One-time, no hidden setup fees).
  * 🌐 **International:** Flat **$15 USD** (One-time flat rate).
* **The "Skin in the Game" Guarantee:** 
  * **₹0 Upfront Advance:** The agency builds the live, working website preview first.
  * **Pay 100% on Approval:** The client only pays after they test the site, see their branding, and are completely satisfied. If they don't love it, they pay ₹0.
* **Hosting Policy:** 
  * Free permanent high-speed cloud hosting on Netlify's global edge CDN via a free `.netlify.app` subdomain. Zero recurring monthly server bills.
* **Custom Domain Policy:** 
  * Custom domains (`.com`, `.in`, `.co.in`, etc.) are **not free** because third-party registrars charge annual fees.
  * Clients who own a domain can connect it for **free**.
  * Clients who want the agency to purchase and configure the domain can inquire for the standard registrar fee (~₹800–₹1,200/yr or ~$10–$14/yr).
* **No Confusing Tiers:**
  * Clean, single flat-price presentation. The 24/7 AI Receptionist is available as an optional turnkey add-on or bundled feature.

### A. Real-Time Currency Switcher (INR ₹ / USD $)
* Interactive header toggle dynamically rewrites DOM currency values, pricing cards, ROI calculations, and pre-filled WhatsApp message strings without page reloads.

### B. Interactive 3-Theme Luxury Engine
* **🌌 Obsidian Black (Default):** Deep luxury void (`#03050C`) with electric indigo (`#6366F1`) and emerald flares (`#10B981`).
* **⚡ Cyber Midnight:** Futuristic deep blue (`#050814`) with neon cyan grid highlights (`#38BDF8`).
* **🍃 Emerald Matrix:** Matrix-inspired dark obsidian (`#020B08`) with mint luminescence (`#34D399`).
* Full `localStorage` persistence and 0.4s smooth CSS variable transitions.

### C. Unified 60fps Cursor Physics & Canvas
* Fixed 700px ambient radial aura and 48px coordinate grid spotlight driven by a single `requestAnimationFrame` loop.
* `.track-card`: Specular radial border highlight that traces cursor proximity angle, plus 3D perspective tilt on hover.
* `.track-magnetic`: Primary CTA buttons pull towards the cursor (6px–10px) with internal specular glare.
* `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` compliance for accessibility.
